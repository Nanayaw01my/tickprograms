Our History

From a Small Idea to a Continental Movement

EcoVerde Foundation was born in 2010 from a conversation between three university friends — Dr. Ama Boateng, Engineer Kwame Darko, and educator Efua Mensah — who were alarmed by the accelerating deforestation and pollution they witnessed in their communities.

What began as weekend tree-planting events in Accra's Achimota Forest quickly attracted hundreds of volunteers and the attention of international environmental bodies. By 2013, EcoVerde was a registered NGO with its first major UNEP grant, allowing a formal launch of the SolarVerde electrification pilot in three rural villages.

Between 2015 and 2019, the organisation expanded into water sanitation, waste management, and climate education, creating a holistic environmental model that now operates across six West African countries. In 2022, EcoVerde received the Africa Green Leadership Award — one of the continent's highest environmental honours.

Today, with a staff of 48, a volunteer base of over 2,000, and partnerships with the UN, WWF, and the African Development Bank, EcoVerde stands as a leading voice for environmental justice and sustainable development.

Our Foundation

Vision, Mission & Objectives

Everything we do is anchored in a clear sense of purpose, direction, and measurable goals.

🔭

Our Vision

A world where every community lives in harmony with a thriving natural environment — where clean energy, clean water, and clean air are universal rights, not privileges, and where future generations inherit a planet richer than the one we found.

🎯

Our Mission

To protect and restore the natural environment while empowering communities to lead their own sustainable development — through renewable energy, reforestation, environmental education, clean water access, and responsible waste management.

❓

Why We Exist

Climate change, deforestation, and pollution disproportionately harm the world's most vulnerable communities. EcoVerde exists because African communities deserve world-class environmental support — and because local knowledge, combined with technical expertise, produces the most durable solutions.

🤝

Our Approach

We work with communities, not for them. Every programme begins with community consultation, co-design, and local capacity building. We combine traditional ecological knowledge with modern science to create solutions that are both effective and culturally resonant.

What We Stand For

Our Core Values

These six principles guide every decision, programme, and partnership we make.

🌱

Sustainability

We design for the long term — every programme must be environmentally, financially, and socially sustainable beyond our direct involvement.

🏘️

Community Ownership

Communities are the architects of their own futures. We facilitate, support, and resource — but local people lead.

🔬

Evidence & Innovation

We invest in research, monitor outcomes rigorously, and embrace new technologies and approaches that improve impact.

⚖️

Environmental Justice

The most vulnerable bear the worst of climate change while contributing the least to it. We actively work to reverse this injustice.

🤲

Integrity & Transparency

We are honest stewards of donor resources. All expenditures are publicly reported and all programmes independently evaluated.

🌐

Collaboration

No single organisation can solve the climate crisis. We actively build partnerships that multiply impact and share knowledge freely.
