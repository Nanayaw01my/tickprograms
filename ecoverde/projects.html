Our Projects & Programs

Six transformative initiatives tackling the most pressing environmental challenges facing African communities today.

Our programs are designed as integrated systems — clean energy enables refrigeration for food security; reforestation improves watershed health for clean water; climate education builds the next generation of environmental stewards. Together, they create a virtuous cycle of sustainability.

Active & Upcoming Initiatives

Active

SolarVerde Rural Electrification

Bringing affordable, clean solar electricity to off-grid households, schools, and health clinics in northern Ghana. Each installation replaces kerosene lamps and diesel generators, cutting household energy costs by up to 80% and reducing carbon emissions significantly.

Current Impact 1,200 households electrified · 6 health clinics · 18 schools · 4,800 tonnes CO₂ saved annually
📍 Northern Region, Upper East, Upper West — Ghana
Active

Green Canopy Reforestation Drive

A community-led reforestation programme targeting severely degraded forest zones. We use a diverse mix of native species — not monocultures — to restore biodiversity, protect watersheds, and sequester carbon. Communities receive training and earn income as forest custodians.

Current Impact 85,000 trees planted · 1,200 hectares restored · 320 community custodians employed
📍 Brong-Ahafo & Western Regions — Ghana
Ongoing

Eco-Schools Climate Curriculum

Partnering with Ghana Education Service to embed climate science, sustainability, and ecological thinking into primary and secondary school curricula. Includes teacher training, eco-garden kits, and an annual inter-school environmental competition.

Current Impact 120 partner schools · 45,000 students reached · 680 teachers trained · 6 regions
📍 Nationwide — Ghana (6 Regions)
Active

ClearWater Community Sanitation

Installing solar-powered boreholes, rainwater harvesting systems, and ecological sanitation facilities in communities where access to safe water is limited. Women and children — who bear the burden of water collection — benefit most directly.

Current Impact 28 water systems installed · 12,000 people with reliable clean water · 3 hours/day saved per household
📍 Northern Ghana & Volta Region
Ongoing

WasteZero Plastic Recovery Programme

A circular economy initiative that collects, sorts, and upcycles plastic waste from urban communities and coastal areas. Waste collectors receive fair wages, plastic is converted into construction materials, and the programme generates sustainable livelihoods.

Current Impact 340 tonnes of plastic diverted · 180 waste collectors employed · 3 upcycling workshops established
📍 Accra, Tema & Takoradi — Ghana
Launching 2025

ClimateFarms Agro-Ecology Initiative

Equipping smallholder farmers with climate-smart agriculture techniques — drought-resistant seed varieties, agroforestry, soil conservation, and water-efficient irrigation — to build resilience against increasingly unpredictable rainfall patterns.

Target Impact 500 farms enrolled · 2,000 farming households supported · 15% average yield increase projected
📍 Northern Ghana, Togo & Burkina Faso
