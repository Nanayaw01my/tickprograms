Our Story
Who We Are

A Decade of Environmental Leadership

EcoVerde Foundation was established in 2010 with a single conviction: that sustainable environmental stewardship and human wellbeing are two sides of the same coin.

Over the past 14 years, we have grown from a grassroots movement in Accra, Ghana into a Pan-African force for environmental change — planting forests, building solar systems, cleaning waterways, educating youth, and mobilising communities to lead their own green futures.

Our multidisciplinary team of environmentalists, engineers, educators, and community organisers works hand-in-hand with local leaders, governments, and international partners to create lasting, scalable impact.
