FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Can I visit your office?

Yes — visitors are welcome Monday to Saturday during office hours. We recommend calling ahead to ensure the right team member is available to meet you.

How quickly do you respond to messages?

We aim to reply to all enquiries within 2 business days. For urgent matters, please call our main line at +233 302 700 400.

Can I request a presentation?

Absolutely. We regularly present to schools, universities, corporations, and government agencies. Email media@ecoverde.org to arrange a session.

Are you open to media interviews?

Yes — contact our Communications Director at media@ecoverde.org. We welcome responsible media coverage of our programmes and environmental issues.
