Support Our Environmental Mission

Every contribution — large or small — plants trees, powers homes, cleans water, and educates the next generation of environmental stewards.

Why Give?

Your Donation Changes Lives

Environmental degradation is not an abstract problem — it is women walking hours for water, children studying by kerosene lamplight, and farmers watching their harvests fail. Your donation is the bridge between these realities and the sustainable futures communities deserve.

EcoVerde is committed to maximising every donation. We publish annual financial reports and independent impact evaluations so you can see exactly where your money goes. Over 84% of all funds go directly to programmes — not overhead.

What Your Donation Achieves

$25USD

Plants 10 Native Trees

Covers seedling, soil prep, planting labour, and 12 months of custodian monitoring for 10 native trees.

$75USD

Supplies an Eco-Schools Kit

Provides one classroom with a full climate education kit including seed trays, soil, tools, and teacher resources.

$200USD

Solar Lamp for 4 Households

Equips four families with quality solar lamps, replacing kerosene and enabling children to study at night safely.

$500USD

Funds a Home Solar System

Covers panels, battery storage, wiring, and installation for one complete household solar electrification.

$2,500USD

Community Water Point

Co-funds a solar-powered borehole providing safe water for an entire community of 200+ people.
