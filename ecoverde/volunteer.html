Join Our Volunteer Community

Your time and skills are among the most powerful gifts you can give to the planet. Thousands of volunteers have shaped EcoVerde's impact — become part of the story.

Why Volunteer?

What You Gain & What You Give

Volunteering with EcoVerde is a two-way exchange — you contribute your energy and skills, and you gain experience, connections, and the deep satisfaction of meaningful work.

🌳

Real Environmental Impact

Every tree you plant, every lesson you teach, and every watt of solar you help install creates measurable, lasting change in the natural world.

🎓

Skills & Certification

Gain hands-on experience in conservation, renewable energy, community development, and environmental education. Receive an EcoVerde Volunteer Certificate.

🤝

Community & Network

Join a diverse, passionate community of 2,000+ volunteers, partner with leading environmentalists, and build lifelong professional connections.

Open Roles

Volunteer Opportunities

We match volunteers to roles that fit their skills, availability, and passion. Here are our current openings:

  • 🌱

    Reforestation Field Volunteer

    Join weekend tree-planting drives in forest zones. No experience needed — just enthusiasm. Tools and transport provided.

  • ☀️

    Solar Installation Assistant

    Support our engineering teams on rural solar installations. Ideal for electrical, engineering, or construction backgrounds.

  • 🏫

    Eco-Schools Educator

    Facilitate climate and sustainability lessons in partner schools. Suitable for teachers, student educators, and science professionals.

  • 💧

    Water & Sanitation Support

    Help with borehole community engagement, hygiene promotion, and maintenance training. WASH experience preferred.

  • ♻️

    WasteZero Collection Volunteer

    Participate in community clean-ups, plastic sorting drives, and awareness campaigns in urban and coastal areas.

  • 📸

    Media & Communications Volunteer

    Document field activities, write blog posts, manage social media, or produce short videos. Creative and digital skills welcome.

  • 📊

    Research & Data Volunteer

    Support our M&E team with field surveys, data entry, biodiversity monitoring, and impact report preparation.
