A Word From Our Pastor

Welcome to Our Church Family

"For I know the plans I have for you," declares the LORD, "plans to prosper you and not to harm you, plans to give you hope and a future." — Jeremiah 29:11

Welcome to Truth Faith Rainbow Ministry — a place where every soul is valued, every story matters, and every heart is invited to encounter the living God. We are a vibrant, Spirit-filled community rooted in the Word of God and committed to love, service, and authentic faith.

Whether you are searching for purpose, seeking healing, or growing deeper in your walk with Christ, you have found a home here. Our doors — and our hearts — are always open to you.

Rev. Samuel Acheampong
Senior Pastor, Truth Faith Rainbow Ministry
