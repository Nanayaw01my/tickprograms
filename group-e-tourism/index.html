Ghana's Central Region — A Land of Heritage & Wonder

The Central Region of Ghana is one of the country's most historically significant and naturally diverse destinations. Bordered by the Gulf of Guinea to the south, it offers a compelling blend of UNESCO World Heritage sites, pristine beaches, lush rainforest, and vibrant local culture.

From the solemn corridors of Cape Coast Castle and Elmina Castle — poignant reminders of the transatlantic slave trade — to the breathtaking canopy walkway of Kakum National Park, the region captivates every kind of traveller.

Experience the warm hospitality of the Fante people, taste fresh seafood along the Atlantic coast, and immerse yourself in centuries of tradition, craft, and ceremony.
