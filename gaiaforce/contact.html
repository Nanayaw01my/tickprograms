FAQ

Common Questions

Can I visit your Accra office?

Yes — visitors are welcome Mon–Sat during office hours. We recommend calling ahead so the right person is available.

How quickly do you reply to messages?

We aim to reply within 2 business days. For urgent matters, call +233 302 880 500 directly.

Can I request a presentation or talk?

Absolutely. We regularly present to schools, universities, corporations, and government agencies. Email media@gaiaforce.org.

Are you open to media interviews?

Yes — email media@gaiaforce.org. We welcome responsible, accurate coverage of our work and environmental issues.
