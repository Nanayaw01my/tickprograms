Our Work

Six Programs.
One Shared Planet.

Integrated environmental programmes that work together to create systemic, lasting change — from the soil to the sky.

Our six programmes are intentionally designed as an integrated ecosystem. Solar energy powers our water boreholes. Healthier forests improve watershed quality. Educated students become the next generation of climate champions. When these systems work together, they create a virtuous cycle of environmental recovery and community resilience.

All Programmes

Active & Upcoming Initiatives

Active

SunForce Rural Solar

Off-grid solar electrification for households, health clinics, and schools across northern Ghana. Each system includes battery storage and a community technician trained in maintenance, ensuring long-term reliability without external dependence.

⚡ 1,800 homes · 6 health clinics · 24 schools · 7,200T CO₂ saved/yr
📍 Northern, Upper East & Upper West 🗓 2014–present
Active

ForestBack Reforestation

Large-scale reforestation using only native species to restore biodiversity, protect watersheds, and sequester carbon. Communities earn income as certified forest custodians — giving them a direct economic stake in the forest's survival.

🌳 120,000 trees · 1,600 ha · 420 custodians · 8 species varieties
📍 Brong-Ahafo & Western Regions 🗓 2016–present
Active

AquaGaia Clean Water

Solar-powered boreholes, rainwater harvesting systems, and ecological sanitation facilities built in partnership with communities that have lived without safe water access. Women and children are central to every design and governance process.

💧 38 systems · 16,000 beneficiaries · 3.5 hrs/day saved per family
📍 Northern & Volta Regions 🗓 2018–present
Ongoing

EduClimate School Programme

Embedding climate science, ecological thinking, and sustainability skills into primary and secondary school curricula. Includes teacher training, eco-garden starter kits, and an annual inter-school environmental championship.

🏫 160 schools · 62,000 students · 800 teachers trained · 8 regions
📍 National (8 Regions) 🗓 2019–present
Ongoing

ZeroWaste Circular Economy

Urban and coastal plastic recovery that pays waste collectors fair wages, upcycles plastics into construction materials and furniture, and runs hygiene-awareness campaigns. Turning the waste problem into a livelihood solution.

♻️ 480 tonnes diverted · 220 collectors employed · 4 upcycle workshops
📍 Accra, Tema, Takoradi 🗓 2020–present
Launching 2025

AgriClimate Smart Farming

Equipping smallholder farmers with drought-resistant seeds, agroforestry techniques, water-efficient irrigation, and soil conservation practices — building resilience against the increasingly erratic rainfall patterns driven by climate change.

🌾 Target: 600 farms · 3,000 families · 18% avg. yield increase projected
📍 N. Ghana, Togo & Burkina Faso 🗓 Launching 2025
