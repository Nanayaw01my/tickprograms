Who We Are

A Force of Nature.
A Force for Good.

Founded in 2012 by a team of scientists, engineers and community organisers, GaiaForce Foundation has grown into one of West Africa's most trusted environmental NGOs — delivering verifiable, community-owned climate solutions at scale.

We don't do charity. We build capacity. Every programme we run transfers knowledge, skills, and ownership to the communities we serve — creating self-sustaining environmental stewardship that outlasts our involvement.

SunForce Rural Solar

Solar-powered boreholes and rainwater harvesting systems delivering safe, year-round water to communities where none existed before.

📍 Northern & Volta Regions 🗓 Since 2018
Proven Impact

Why Our Model Works

GaiaForce's secret is community ownership. We co-design every programme with the people it will serve, train locals as technicians and educators, and hand over full operational control once communities are ready.

The result? Projects that thrive long after our teams leave — because communities built them, own them, and profit from them.

  • ✓ Evidence-based, independently evaluated programmes
  • ✓ 87% of funds go directly to field operations
  • ✓ Community-led governance at every project site
  • ✓ Published annual impact reports since 2013
