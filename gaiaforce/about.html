Our History

From a Garage to a Movement

GaiaForce Foundation was born in 2012 from the frustration of three scientists who kept publishing environmental research that nobody was acting on. Dr. Esi Darko, Engineer Nana Acheampong, and educator Yaa Boakye decided to stop writing reports and start building solutions.

Their first project — a 200-tree community planting event in the Volta Region — attracted 80 volunteers and media attention that neither expected. Within months, they had registered a legal entity, attracted their first grant from UNEP, and begun a solar pilot in Bawku.

Today, GaiaForce is a 55-person organisation with operations in six West African countries, a volunteer base of 2,400+, and partnerships with the world's leading environmental institutions. But the original spirit — scrappy, science-led, community-first — hasn't changed.

Our Compass

Vision, Mission & Purpose

🔭

Our Vision

A world where every African community thrives in a healthy natural environment — where clean energy, safe water, and living forests are universal rights, not privileges.

🎯

Our Mission

To protect and restore ecosystems while empowering communities to lead their own sustainable futures — through renewable energy, reforestation, clean water, education, and climate-smart livelihoods.

❓

Why We Exist

Environmental degradation hits the most vulnerable hardest. GaiaForce exists because those communities deserve world-class solutions built with them — not imposed on them.

What We Stand For

Core Values

These six principles are non-negotiable — they shape who we hire, how we work, and how we measure success.

🌱

Sustainability First

Every solution we build must be environmentally, financially, and socially sustainable beyond our direct involvement.

🏘️

Community Ownership

Locals lead. We facilitate, resource, and transfer — until communities no longer need us.

🔬

Evidence & Innovation

We invest in rigorous research, embrace new technology, and publish our findings openly for the global environmental community.

⚖️

Environmental Justice

The world's poorest bear the worst of climate change while contributing least to it. We actively work to reverse this injustice.

🔍

Radical Transparency

Every dollar is publicly tracked. Every programme is independently evaluated. We hide nothing.

🌐

Collaborative Power

No single organisation solves the climate crisis. We build genuine partnerships and share knowledge freely.
