Why Volunteer?

Give Time. Gain Everything.

🌍

Verifiable Impact

Every hour you volunteer is tracked against measurable environmental outcomes — trees planted, homes solarised, students taught. You'll know exactly what you achieved.

🎓

Skills & Certification

Gain hands-on experience in conservation, renewable energy, WASH, or education. Receive an official GaiaForce Volunteer Certificate recognised across the sector.

🌐

Global Network

Join a 2,400-strong community of environmental changemakers. Build professional connections with scientists, engineers, policy experts, and community leaders.

Open Roles

Volunteer Opportunities

We match volunteers to roles that fit their skills, location, and schedule.

🌱

Reforestation Field Volunteer

Weekend planting drives. No experience needed — tools, training, and lunch provided.

☀️

Solar Installation Assistant

Support engineering teams on rural installations. Electrical or construction background preferred.

🏫

EduClimate Classroom Facilitator

Run climate lessons in partner schools. Ideal for teachers, graduates, and science professionals.

💧

AquaGaia Community Liaison

Facilitate community engagement for water system installations and maintenance training.

♻️

ZeroWaste Collection Volunteer

Join community clean-ups and plastic sorting drives in urban and coastal areas.

📸

Media & Communications

Document field activities, write stories, manage social media, or produce short films.

📊

Research & Data Volunteer

Support field surveys, biodiversity monitoring, data entry, and impact reporting.
