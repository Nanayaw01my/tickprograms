About Flight Delight

More than a restaurant — a celebration of Ghanaian culture, community and cuisine in the heart of Sunyani.

Our Story

Born in Sunyani,
Built with Passion

Flight Delight was founded in 2010 by Chef Emmanuel Boateng, a Sunyani native who dreamed of creating a space where Ghanaian food was celebrated without compromise — no shortcuts, no imitations, just pure, soulful cooking passed down through generations.

What began as a small chop bar on Asuah Road has grown into one of the most beloved dining destinations in the Bono Region. Our kitchen fuses traditional Ghanaian recipes with modern culinary techniques, delivering dishes that honour our roots while delighting contemporary palates.

Today, Flight Delight seats over 120 guests, employs 35 passionate staff, and continues to grow — driven by the same simple belief that started it all: great food brings people together.
