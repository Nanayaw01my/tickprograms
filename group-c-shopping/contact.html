❓ Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers to our most common customer enquiries

How do I track my order? +
Once your order is dispatched, you will receive an SMS and email with a tracking number. You can use this to track your package on our website or the courier's portal. Alternatively, call our support line with your Order ID for a real-time update.
What payment methods do you accept? +
We accept MTN Mobile Money, Vodafone Cash (Telecel Money), AirtelTigo Money, Visa, Mastercard, and bank transfers. We also offer Pay on Delivery in select Accra and Kumasi locations for orders under ₵2,000.
How long does delivery take? +
Standard delivery to Accra takes 1–2 business days. Kumasi, Tamale, and Cape Coast take 2–3 business days. Other regions may take 3–5 business days. Express same-day delivery is available in Accra for orders placed before 12pm (Monday–Saturday).
What is your return and refund policy? +
We offer a 14-day return window on most products. Items must be unused, in original packaging with all accessories. Electronics that develop a fault within 30 days are covered by our SmartBuy warranty. Refunds are processed within 3–5 working days via the original payment method.
Are the products on SmartBuy Ghana authentic? +
Yes, 100%. All electronics sold on SmartBuy Ghana are sourced directly from authorised distributors and come with original manufacturer warranties. Our fashion products are curated from verified Ghanaian and international brands. We have a zero-tolerance policy for counterfeit goods.
Can I become a seller on SmartBuy Ghana? +
Absolutely! We welcome individual sellers, SMEs, and established businesses. Register as a seller through the "Sell on SmartBuy" link in our footer, and our onboarding team will reach out within 48 hours. Requirements include a valid Ghana business registration, a national ID, and product photos.
Do you offer bulk or wholesale pricing? +
Yes! We offer special pricing for bulk orders (10+ units). Contact our B2B team at wholesale@smartbuyghana.com or call +233 54 987 6543 with your requirements. We serve schools, businesses, government institutions, and NGOs across Ghana.
