Our Mission & Vision

Guided by a clear purpose, we strive to be the leading computer science department in West Africa.

🎯

Our Mission

To provide a world-class computer science education that fosters critical thinking, innovation, and ethical practice. We are committed to developing technically proficient graduates who can solve real-world problems through research, collaboration, and cutting-edge technology. We nurture a diverse and inclusive community where every student is empowered to reach their full potential.

📄

Our Vision

To be a globally recognised centre of excellence in computing education and research, driving technological innovation for national development and societal advancement. We envision a future where our graduates lead digital transformation across industries, contribute breakthrough research, and uphold the highest standards of professional and academic integrity.

Welcome from the HOD

On behalf of the entire Department of Computer Science, I warmly welcome you to our academic home. Since our establishment in 1994, we have remained steadfast in our commitment to producing graduates who are not only technically competent but also ethically grounded and socially responsible.

Our department boasts state-of-the-art laboratories, a highly qualified faculty, and a vibrant student community. We offer a rich blend of theory and practice, ensuring our students are industry-ready from day one.

  • State-of-the-art computing laboratories
  • Industry partnerships with leading tech companies
  • Active research groups in AI, Cybersecurity & Data Science
  • Annual hackathons, coding competitions & tech fairs
  • Strong alumni network across Africa and beyond
