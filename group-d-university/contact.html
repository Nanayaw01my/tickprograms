Frequently Asked Questions
How do I apply for admission?
Visit our online admissions portal at admissions.groupd.edu.gh or contact our admissions office directly.
When does the academic year start?
Semester One typically begins in early September. Registration usually opens two weeks prior.
Are there scholarships available?
Yes. We offer merit-based and need-based scholarships. Contact the financial aid office for details.
Can I transfer from another institution?
Transfer applications are considered on a case-by-case basis. Contact the Admissions Office for guidance.
