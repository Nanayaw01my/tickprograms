Prof. Kwame Asante-Mensah

🏫 Head of Department & Professor of Computer Science

Prof. Asante-Mensah holds a PhD in Artificial Intelligence from the University of Edinburgh and has over 20 years of academic and research experience. He leads the department's strategic vision, oversees curriculum development, and heads the AI Research Group. He has published over 60 peer-reviewed articles and received numerous national and international awards for excellence in computing education.

PhD AI – Univ. of Edinburgh MSc CS – Imperial College BSc CS – KNUST FICS (Fellow)
