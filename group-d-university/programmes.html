Our Programmes

We offer accredited, internationally recognised programmes at multiple levels — from HND through to PhD — all designed with industry relevance at their core.

Undergraduate Programmes

4-year degree programmes leading to a Bachelor of Science award

BSc

Computer Science

🕐 4 Years  |  Full-time

Our flagship programme equips students with a rigorous foundation in computer science theory, algorithms, and systems programming alongside practical software development skills.

  • Introduction to Programming (Python/C++)
  • Data Structures & Algorithms
  • Operating Systems
  • Database Systems
  • Computer Networks
  • Artificial Intelligence
  • Software Engineering
  • Final Year Research Project

📄 Entry: WASSCE with minimum of C6 in Mathematics & Core English, plus 3 electives

Intake: September Accredited
BSc

Software Engineering

🕐 4 Years  |  Full-time

Focuses on the systematic design, development, testing, and maintenance of large-scale software systems. Combines software process models with hands-on project work.

  • Software Requirements Engineering
  • Object-Oriented Design & UML
  • Agile & DevOps Practices
  • Mobile Application Development
  • Web Technologies (Full Stack)
  • Software Testing & QA
  • Cloud Computing & Microservices
  • Capstone Industry Project

📄 Entry: WASSCE with minimum of C6 in Mathematics & Core English, plus 3 electives

Intake: September Accredited
BSc

Cybersecurity & Digital Forensics

🕐 4 Years  |  Full-time

Addresses the growing global demand for cybersecurity professionals. Students learn to design secure systems, conduct threat analysis, and investigate digital crimes.

  • Network Security Fundamentals
  • Ethical Hacking & Penetration Testing
  • Digital Forensics Investigation
  • Cryptography & Public Key Infrastructure
  • Security Policy & Governance
  • Incident Response & Recovery
  • Malware Analysis
  • Industry Internship (6 months)

📄 Entry: WASSCE with minimum of C6 in Mathematics & Core English, plus 3 electives

Intake: September Accredited

Higher National Diploma (HND) Programmes

2-year programmes leading to the award of Higher National Diploma

HND

Computer Science

🕐 2 Years  |  Full-time

A practical, skills-based programme covering core computing concepts, programming, and information systems. Ideal for students seeking a rapid pathway into industry.

  • Computer Fundamentals & Architecture
  • Programming Essentials (Python)
  • Database Design & SQL
  • Web Design & Development
  • Networking Basics
  • IT Project Management

📄 Entry: WASSCE with minimum of D7 in Mathematics & passes in 3 electives

Intake: September / January Accredited
HND

Information Technology

🕐 2 Years  |  Full-time

Focuses on the application of IT in business environments. Covers systems analysis, enterprise software, IT support, and digital communication.

  • Systems Analysis & Design
  • Enterprise Resource Planning
  • IT Infrastructure & Support
  • E-Commerce & Digital Marketing
  • Spreadsheet & Database Applications
  • Professional IT Practice

📄 Entry: WASSCE with minimum of D7 in Mathematics & passes in 3 electives

Intake: September / January Accredited

Postgraduate Programmes

Advanced academic and research degrees

MSc

Computer Science

🕐 2 Years  |  Full-time / Part-time

A research-intensive Master's programme allowing students to specialise in Artificial Intelligence, Data Science, Networks, or Information Security.

  • Advanced Algorithms & Complexity
  • Machine Learning & Deep Learning
  • Big Data Analytics
  • Research Methodology
  • Specialisation Electives
  • Master's Thesis / Dissertation

📄 Entry: BSc Computer Science (2nd Class Lower or above) or equivalent

Intake: September Accredited
PhD

Computer Science

🕐 3–5 Years  |  Full-time

Our doctoral programme offers candidates the opportunity to conduct original research that advances the frontiers of computer science under the supervision of world-class faculty.

  • Doctoral Research Seminars
  • Advanced Topics in Computing
  • Peer-Reviewed Publication (minimum 2)
  • International Conference Presentations
  • PhD Thesis & Oral Defence

📄 Entry: MSc in Computer Science or closely related field

Rolling Intake Accredited
