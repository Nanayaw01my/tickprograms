Featured Event 📅 June 28, 2026

Annual Department Tech Fair & Hackathon 2026 — Open for Registration

The Department of Computer Science is proud to announce the 12th Annual Tech Fair and 24-Hour Hackathon, scheduled for Saturday, 28th June 2026 at the Main Auditorium. This flagship event brings together students, faculty, industry professionals, and entrepreneurs for a day of innovation, competition, and networking.

This year's theme is "Artificial Intelligence for Sustainable Development in Africa". Teams of up to four students will compete for prizes totalling GHS 50,000, sponsored by Google, MTN Ghana, and Vodafone Ghana. Registration is free and open to all students.

Dr. Efua Mensah Wins Africa Excellence Research Award in Data Science

The department congratulates Dr. Efua Mensah, Senior Lecturer in Data Science, on winning the prestigious Africa Excellence Research Award 2026. Her groundbreaking work on applying Natural Language Processing to under-resourced Ghanaian languages has received international acclaim.

Partnership 📅 May 22, 2026

Department Signs MoU with Google for Student Developer Scholarship Programme

Group D University's Department of Computer Science has formalised a Memorandum of Understanding with Google Africa to sponsor 50 students annually through the Google Developer Scholarship programme. The partnership also includes guest lectures, research grants, and on-campus recruitment drives.

Achievement 📅 May 5, 2026

Class of 2026: 312 Students Graduate with Distinction in Record Numbers

The 2025/2026 academic year produced a record 312 graduates from the Department of Computer Science, with 48 students graduating with First Class Honours. The department also celebrated its first-ever PhD graduands — three students who successfully defended their doctoral theses in March 2026.

Infrastructure 📅 April 18, 2026

New State-of-the-Art Cybersecurity Laboratory Commissioned on Campus

The Vice-Chancellor officially commissioned the new Cybersecurity and Digital Forensics Laboratory on 18th April 2026. Funded through a GHS 2.4 million grant from the Ghana Ministry of Education, the lab is equipped with enterprise-grade security tools, penetration testing rigs, and forensic workstations for 40 students.
