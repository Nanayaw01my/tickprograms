Hello! I'm Kwame Mensah

I'm a passionate web designer and front-end developer based in Accra, Ghana. I recently graduated with a BSc in Computer Science and have spent the last three years honing my craft — turning concepts into clean, functional, and beautiful websites.

My journey started in my first year of university when I built a simple HTML page for a friend's small business. Watching a design come to life in the browser was instantly addictive. Since then I've worked on over 15 projects, ranging from personal portfolios and small-business sites to landing pages and e-commerce storefronts.

I believe great design is invisible — it gets out of the way and lets the content speak. I focus on accessibility, performance, and pixel-perfect implementation of designs. When I'm not coding, you'll find me exploring typography, sketching UI ideas, or playing football with friends.

I'm currently available for freelance projects and collaborations. If you have an idea you'd like to bring to the web, let's talk.
