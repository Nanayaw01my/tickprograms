Property Description

Welcome to this stunning luxury villa nestled in the prestigious neighbourhood of East Legon, Accra. Built in 2021 to the highest specifications, this magnificent 5-bedroom family home offers an unrivalled combination of space, elegance, and modern convenience.

On the ground floor, you are greeted by a grand entrance foyer leading into a sweeping open-plan living and dining area with soaring ceilings and polished marble floors. The chef's kitchen is fully fitted with imported German cabinetry, granite countertops, and top-of-the-range appliances. A guest bedroom with en-suite and a separate study complete the ground level.

Upstairs, the master suite is a sanctuary of comfort, featuring a walk-in dressing room, spa-inspired en-suite bathroom, and a private balcony overlooking the landscaped garden and swimming pool. Three additional bedrooms each have built-in wardrobes and en-suite facilities. The landscaped exterior boasts a heated swimming pool, a covered entertainment terrace, mature tropical gardens, and a secure double garage.
