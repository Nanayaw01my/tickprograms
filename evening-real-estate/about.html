Our Story

Built on Trust, Driven by Excellence

Prime Homes Ghana was founded in 2012 by Nana Kweku Asante and Maame Efua Darko with a single, powerful vision: to make quality property ownership accessible to every Ghanaian — regardless of where they live, whether at home or in the diaspora.

What started as a two-person agency in a shared office in Osu, Accra, has grown into one of Ghana's most recognised and respected real estate brands, with over 60 licensed agents, three regional offices, and more than 1,200 active listings spanning Accra, Kumasi, Takoradi, Tamale, and Ho.

"We don't just sell properties — we help Ghanaians build futures. Every transaction we handle is a family's dream, and we treat it that way."
— Nana Kweku Asante, Co-Founder & CEO

Over 14 years, we have facilitated more than 4,500 successful property transactions, helped diaspora investors navigate Ghanaian property law, and earned the trust of government institutions, corporate clients, and individual homebuyers alike.

Our Core Values

These principles guide every interaction, every listing, and every transaction we undertake.

🔒

Integrity & Transparency

We believe in honest communication at every stage. No hidden fees, no misleading listings. Every property we list has been verified by our legal and inspection team.

🌟

Client-First Excellence

Your needs shape our process. Whether you are a first-time buyer or a seasoned investor, we tailor our services to deliver the best possible outcome for you.

🇬🇭

Ghanaian Pride

We are proud to be a Ghanaian company that supports local communities, champions land rights, and contributes to the sustainable development of our cities.

💡

Innovation

We embrace technology — from virtual property tours to digital title deed verification — to make the property buying process faster and more convenient for all clients.

🤝

Long-Term Relationships

We don't disappear after the deal is done. Our clients return to us again and again because we continue to provide market insights, property management, and after-sales support.

Expertise & Education

We invest heavily in our agents' professional development and run free first-time buyer seminars quarterly to empower Ghanaians with the knowledge to make smart property decisions.
